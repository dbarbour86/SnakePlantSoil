# Ultimate Winter Watering Guide

Winter is the most dangerous season for snake plants. As the days shorten and temperatures drop, snake plants enter a state of **dormancy**. During this time, their metabolic rate slows down to almost zero, and their roots consume very little water.

If you continue watering them on their summer schedule, the soil will remain damp for weeks, leading to **root rot**.

## The Golden Rules of Winter Watering

- **Water Only Every 6-8 Weeks**: Indoor snake plants during winter only need water once every month and a half, or even every two months.
- **Check the Soil Depth**: The soil must be 100% dry from the top to the very bottom of the pot. Use a wood chopstick to test.
- **Use Room Temperature Water**: Cold water can shock the dormant roots.

> Always remember: it is far better to underwater a snake plant than to overwater it. A dry snake plant can survive for months; an overwatered one can die in weeks.
